The Britain we’re building

Six promises. Yes, we have a plan.

Imagine a Britain where your rent builds a home you own, and your work builds savings that are yours. Where no one is one bad month away from losing everything, and no one grows rich just by sitting on what they already have. Where the things we all depend on, from energy and water to the trains and the NHS, are run for the people who use them. Where politics answers to voters, not donors, and your rights do not depend on being in the majority. A country that makes things, and shares what it makes.

How we pay for it

A government is not a household. It creates the pounds it spends, and tax takes pounds back out, so that spending doesn’t push up prices. The real limit is what Britain can make and do, not money.

We tax what people take, not what they make. The first £20,000 you earn is tax-free, and National Insurance goes. The bill moves to land, large fortunes and the biggest businesses.

  • From year 1: close loopholes, tax gains from wealth the same as wages, a minimum tax on fortunes above £5 million, and a surcharge on share buybacks.
  • From years 2–5: one Business Value Tax replaces VAT, corporation tax and employers’ National Insurance. A land tax replaces business rates. A fairer property tax replaces council tax and stamp duty.
  • Throughout: an independent Fiscal Council checks the numbers, and an Inflation Brake in law tells the government what to do if prices rise too fast.

For every step in the plan, we will publish what it costs and how we keep prices steady while we do it. Our costings are best estimates, and we show our working so you can check them.
