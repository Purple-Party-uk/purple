What we believe

Reward making, not owning

Tax and regulation should favour people who build businesses, do the work and make things, not people who collect rent on what they already own.

Fix the structure, don't police behaviour

Where the system lets money be drained out, we remove the mechanism rather than write rules asking people not to use it.

Clear rules

Thresholds everyone can understand and that can be enforced, rather than case-by-case judgement that favours those who can afford lawyers.

Honest numbers

Every policy comes with our best estimate of what it costs. Where we are unsure of a figure, we say so. Where we are wrong, we correct it in public.

The same for everyone

Schemes that apply to everyone are simpler to run and harder to game than means-testing, and people can see what they are entitled to.

Not left, not right

The question we ask of every policy is not whether it is left or right, but whether it rewards people for creating value or lets others extract it. Some of our answers will sound left-wing, others right-wing. That is the point.

How our policies are made

Every policy is written in plain English and published in full, with our best estimate of what it costs. We don’t have the Treasury’s models, so some of those estimates will be wrong. Where a policy needs a new law, we draft the bill and publish that too, so you can see exactly what we would do rather than what we say we would do.

Every policy is open for comment. If you think we have got something wrong, tell us on the policy page.
