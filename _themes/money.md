Most people work hard and still find that wages barely cover the bills. Then they look at the tax system and cannot tell what they pay, or why. Wages carry income tax and National Insurance. Money made from owning shares or property is taxed under separate rules, often at lower rates. The people with the most can afford advisers to work the gaps. Everyone else pays in full.

Purple starts from one principle. A pound is a pound, however you got it. Income from work and income from owning things should be taxed the same way, and the tax should be simple enough for anyone to check.

So we would replace income tax, National Insurance, capital gains tax and dividend tax with one income tax, with the first £20,000 tax-free for everyone. VAT would come off everyday goods and stay on luxuries. Council Tax and Stamp Duty would become one fair tax on what homes are worth today. Inheritance would be taxed on what each person receives over their lifetime, with family homes and working farms protected from forced sales. And nobody would fall below a guaranteed income floor.
