## What we would do

We would set public spending on research and development at £165 billion a year. Of that, £150 billion would go to civilian research and £15 billion to defence research.

The increase would be phased in over five years. The budget would then rise each year in line with inflation, measured by the Consumer Prices Index, so that it keeps its value.
